06 it survives

Knock it over. It gets up.

Kill it mid-task and it continues after restart, without sending the same message twice.*

* On Telegram, a crash mid-send can repeat it.

its journalevery step, on disk

  1. read acme.example/pricing
  2. ran compare-pricing.mjs
  3. sent to #pricing
  4. process killed
  5. restarted, continues here
  6. message already went out: not sent again
  7. 17:00 follow-up
kill -9

09 quick start

Its first day: three steps.

  1. step 1

    Install it

  2. step 2

    Make your bot

    Give it a name, a model and one sentence about its job.

  3. step 3

    Invite it to your channels

    Tell it “join our Slack” in pots chat. It sets the app up itself.

Needs Node.js 24+. macOS, Linux, Windows.

It gets full control of the computer it runs on. Give it a computer or VM of its own.
